Based on the Alerting and Detection Strategy (ADS) framework.

* **Goal:** what behavior this detects.
* **ATT&CK:** Txxxx
* **Data sources:** tables / log sources required.
* **Strategy abstract:** how the logic works in two or three sentences.
* **Technical context:** what the analyst needs to understand the alert.
* **Blind spots and assumptions:** what this rule will miss.
